§3. FIXED-STEPS PROBE (isolate envelope vs march)
================================================================================
Purpose: If j0 gap is proportional to iteration count → trip-count tax.
         If j0 gap is constant regardless of steps → envelope/pipeline tax.
Method: Force VTK_METAL_TEST_MARCH_STEPS=X, measure j0 at 2048.
Frames: 15, reps: 1.

Steps   GL (ms)  Metal (ms)  M/GL
  1      40.19     44.48     1.107
  5      39.55     44.26     1.119
 10      46.18     44.36     0.961
 20      43.47     44.59     1.026
 50      43.34     46.06     1.063
100      43.38     44.23     1.020
200      41.34     44.21     1.069
400      41.12     44.47     1.082
1000     42.83     44.72     1.044

Key observations:
  1. Metal time is CONSTANT (~44.5ms) from steps=1 to steps=1000.
     The march loop adds ~0.2ms total over the entire range.
     Per-sample cost ≈ 0.0002ms (0.2μs). Negligible.

  2. GL time varies 39.5-46.2ms (thermal noise), averaging ~42.5ms.

  3. M/GL j0 is ~1.02-1.11 across ALL step counts. The ratio does NOT
     scale with march work. At steps=1 (near-zero march), Metal is STILL
     10.7% slower.

  4. The ~4ms absolute gap (44.5 - 40.5) is present at steps=1 where the
     march is essentially a no-op.

Verdict: j0 gap is NOT a trip-count or per-sample cost. It is a fixed
         envelope cost that exists BEFORE the march loop runs.

§4. J0 GAP CLASSIFICATION
================================================================================
Done criteria from the plan:

  [x] §1 interleaved rounds at 2048: M/GL = 1.046 ± 0.029
      → NOT 1.00 ± 0.08. j0 is not pure noise.

  [x] §3 fixed-steps probe: gap CONSTANT across step counts 1-1000
      → NOT proportional to samples. Not a trip-count hole.

  [x] §3 steps=1: M/GL = 1.11 (Metal still ~10% slower with 1 sample)
      → Gap SURVIVES a near-zero-march probe.

  CONCLUSION: Done criterion #3 — "gap survives a no-march probe"
  → Freeze as ENVELOPE TAX, not a shader issue.

Classification: **C. j0 = envelope (~4ms fixed)**

The ~4ms Metal overhead sits in:
  - Render pass / framebuffer setup (MTLStorageModePrivate offscreen RT)
  - Command buffer encoding / commit overhead
  - VTK pipeline differences (depth attach, matrix setup)
  - Possibly Metal's inherent per-frame CPU-side encoding cost

This is NOT:
  - Trip-count (ruled out by fixed-steps)
  - Per-sample 3D texture fetch cost (ruled out by steps=1)
  - Depth/clip differences (steps=1 bypasses march entirely)

§5. IMPACT ON JITTER INVESTIGATION
================================================================================
Per the plan's "State C" (j0 = envelope):

  "Subtract 4 ms from both Metal j0 and Metal j1; spread is unchanged.
   That is the whole point of doing j0 first: a fixed tax CANNOT produce
   Metal Δ = 2× GL Δ."

This is confirmed. The jitter asymmetry (Metal Δ ≈ 2× GL Δ) is entirely
a j1 phenomenon. The j0 envelope tax adds ~4ms to both j0 and j1, but
does NOT affect the jitter delta:

  Metal Δ = Metal j1 - Metal j0 = (Metal_envelope + Metal_march + Metal_jitter)
                          - (Metal_envelope + Metal_march)
                          = Metal_jitter

  GL Δ = GL j1 - GL j0 = GL_jitter

The envelope cancels out in the jitter delta. So:
  Metal Δ ≈ +25ms, GL Δ ≈ +13ms → jitter asymmetry is ~2×
  This is PURELY a jitter (per-pixel field divergence) issue.

Frozen metrics for jitter investigation:
  M/GL j0: 1.05 (envelope tax, ~4ms, do not re-litigate)
  Metal Δ: ~25ms (jitter cost)
  GL Δ:    ~13ms (jitter cost)
  spread:  (M/GL j1) - (M/GL j0) = jitter with j0 divided out

§6. RECOMMENDATION
================================================================================
Freeze j0 as envelope tax. Do NOT:
  - Add Metal iteration PPM (unnecessary — steps=1 proves march is irrelevant)
  - Modify march shaders to fix j0

DO:

  - At 1024 (M/GL j0 ≈ 1.00), spread is the pure jitter story
  - At 2048 (M/GL j0 ≈ 1.05), subtract envelope before comparing jitter
